Terms & Policies

Everything we make is handmade, so a few house rules keep things smooth for both of us. Short version below — nothing hidden.

Shipping & Returns

How your piece gets made, moved and (if needed) made right.

01

Made just for you

Every piece is handmade to order. Please allow up to 30 days for production (macramé is usually quicker, up to 20). Busy seasons can stretch this a little.

02

On its way

A courier partner carries your parcel. Delivery dates are honest estimates — weather, holidays and customs can nudge them. Once it leaves our hands, transit delays sit with the courier.

03

Pay first, then we begin

We're a small studio, so orders are 100% prepaid by bank transfer. No cash on delivery. Your order starts once your payment is confirmed.

04

All sales are final

Because each piece is made for you, we can't cancel, exchange or take back an order for a change of mind. Unsure? Email us first — we'll happily send more photos or videos.

05

If something isn't right

Damaged or wrong piece? Send one unedited unboxing video within 24 hours of delivery: sealed parcel, label, opening, the item. Damaged pieces get a free repair; wrong pieces get store credit once we've checked the return. You cover return shipping, plus a ₹500 handling & inspection fee. International orders aren't eligible for repair.

06

Lost in transit?

A late or stuck tracking page doesn't mean lost. We'll open a courier investigation, and if the loss is confirmed you'll get store credit for the product value (not shipping or taxes). No cash refunds or replacement shipments. Please keep all packaging until a claim is closed.

07

Refused or returned parcels

If a parcel comes back because of a wrong address, a refusal or missed deliveries, there's no refund and re-sending is at your cost. Double-check your address and phone number!

08

Duties & customs

Within India, taxes are already in the price. For international orders, duties and import charges follow what's shown at checkout.

09

About store credit

Only valid on our website, and can't be swapped for cash.

10

Care & skin

Keep pieces away from water, perfume, lotion and sunlight, and take them off to bathe, swim, work out or sleep. Small variations in beads, knots and finish are part of handmade charm, not defects. We can't promise nickel-free, so take care if your skin is sensitive. Small parts — keep away from children and pets.

Privacy

What we keep, why we keep it, and who sees it.

01

What we collect

Your name, email, phone, delivery & billing address, order and customisation details, plus light, anonymous site analytics (clicks, carts, purchases, a random session ID). We don't intentionally collect IP addresses.

02

Why

To make and ship your order, answer your messages, and make the shop better. That's it.

03

Who sees it

Our courier, payment partners (Wise and Xflow Pay for international transfers) and Supabase, which hosts accounts. They only get what they need. We never sell or rent your data.

04

Payments

We never store your passwords or full card or bank credentials — and please don't email them to us.

05

Marketing

Only if you opt in. Unsubscribe any time; ordering never depends on it.

06

Cookies

Just the basics, for your cart, preferences and simple analytics. You can block them in your browser, but your cart may stop working.

07

Adults only

Our shop is for people 18 and over. Under 18? Please ask a parent or guardian to order for you.

08

Your rights

Ask to see, fix or delete your data, or withdraw consent, any time at relations@plumelleatellie.com. We keep only what the law or our records require.

09

Staying safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, though no website can promise perfect security.
