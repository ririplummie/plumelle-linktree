01

Made to Order — Production Timeline

Every piece is handmade to order. Please allow up to 30 days for production (macramé is usually quicker, up to 20 days). Peak seasons may extend this timeline; we will notify you of any material delay.

02

Courier Partner & Transit

We ship via a third-party courier partner. Delivery dates are estimates and not guaranteed. Once handed to the courier, transit timelines are governed by the courier's terms. Weather, holidays and customs may cause delays.

03

Prepaid Orders Only

As a small studio, all orders are 100% prepaid via bank transfer or approved online payment methods. Cash on Delivery is not available. Production begins only after payment is verified and confirmed.

04

Address Accuracy

You are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address and phone number. If a parcel is returned due to an incorrect address, refusal, or missed delivery, no refund will be issued and re-shipping will be at your cost.

05

International Orders — Duties & Customs

For orders within India, applicable taxes are included in the price. For international orders, any duties, import fees or customs charges levied at the destination are the recipient's responsibility, unless expressly stated otherwise at checkout.

06

Risk of Loss

Risk of loss or damage passes to you upon delivery to the courier. However, if a parcel is confirmed lost in transit by the courier's investigation, you will receive store credit for the product value (excluding shipping and taxes). No cash refunds or replacement shipments will be issued. Please retain all packaging until any claim is closed.

01

Cancellation Policy

All orders are made to order and personalised. Once an order is placed and payment is confirmed, it cannot be cancelled. If you are unsure before ordering, please contact us for additional photos or videos.

02

Return Policy

Because each piece is custom-made, we do not accept returns for change of mind. Returns are only accepted for damaged or incorrectly shipped items, subject to the conditions below.

03

Refund Policy — Store Credit

Approved claims will be resolved via store credit only, not cash refunds. Store credit is issued within 5–7 business days of claim approval. Credit is valid only on our website and cannot be exchanged for cash.

04

Damaged or Wrong Item Claims

You must submit one unedited unboxing video within 24 hours of delivery, showing: the sealed parcel, shipping label, the opening process, and the item. Damaged items are eligible for a free repair (domestic orders only); wrong items are eligible for store credit after we receive and inspect the return.

05

Return Shipping & Handling Fee

For approved returns, you are responsible for return shipping costs plus a ₹500 handling and inspection fee. This fee will be deducted from any store credit issued. International orders are not eligible for repair or return.

06

Refund Processing Timeline

Once an approved return is received and inspected, store credit will be issued within 5–7 business days. You will receive an email confirmation with your credit details.

07

Lost in Transit

If the courier confirms loss in transit, you will receive store credit for the product value (excluding shipping and taxes) within 5–7 business days of claim closure. No cash refunds or replacement shipments will be issued.

02

Purpose of Collection

Your data is used solely to: (a) process and ship your order; (b) respond to your enquiries; (c) improve our website and services. We do not use your data for any other purpose without your consent.

Payment Terms & Security

How payments work and how we protect your data.

Accepted Payment Methods

We accept payments via bank transfer and approved online payment methods as displayed at checkout. All orders are 100% prepaid. Cash on Delivery is not available.

02

Payment Processing

Payments are processed by third-party payment partners (including Wise and Xflow Pay for international transfers). These partners are responsible for the secure processing of your payment information.

03

Card Data Security & Tokenisation

We do not store your complete card number, CVV, or bank credentials on our servers. Card payments are processed via tokenisation in accordance with RBI guidelines — your card details are replaced with a secure token, and the actual card information is never stored by us.

04

Payment Confirmation

Your order will not enter production until payment is verified and confirmed. If payment fails or is reversed, the order will not be processed.

05

Refunds to Payment Method

Where a refund is approved (e.g., if we cannot fulfil a paid order), it will be processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days, unless otherwise agreed. Store credit is the default resolution for product-related claims, as outlined in our Refund Policy.

06

Fraud Prevention

We reserve the right to refuse or cancel any order suspected of fraud, unauthorised payment, or abuse. We may request additional verification before processing such orders.

07

Payment Gateway Disclaimer

Our payment gateway and payment aggregator partners are independent third parties. They are not responsible for product delivery, quality, or customer service. All product-related disputes must be directed to us at relations@plumelleatellie.com.
